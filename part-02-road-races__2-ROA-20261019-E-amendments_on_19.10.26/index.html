    MEMORANDUM

    03.10.2025

    PART II - ROAD RACES

    Rules amendments applying on 19.10.2026

    Chapter X UCI RANKINGS

    § 1 Elite and Under 23 Men’s UCI World Ranking

    Special Provisions for Riders Competing in Other Cycling Disciplines 2.10.004 As from season 2027, points earned at the UCI World Championships in Track, Quarter Mountain Bike XCO, Cyclo-Cross, and Gravel, as well as in the final standings of the UCI World Cup or UCI Nations Cup in Track, Mountain Bike XCO, and Cyclo-Cross, may be added to the UCI Road Team Ranking based on a specific points scale.

    These additional points are included in the team ranking only if the rider is among the top 20 riders within his team’s Men Elite ranking. No points from these disciplines are added to the individual UCI Road Rankings.

    (article introduced on 19.10.26).

    § 2 Elite and Under 23 Women’s UCI World Rankings

    Special Provisions for Riders Competing in Other Cycling Disciplines 2.10.014 As from season 2027, points earned at the UCI World Championships in Track, Bis Mountain Bike XCO, Cyclo-Cross, and Gravel, as well as in the final standings

    of the UCI World Cup or UCI Nations Cup in Track, Mountain Bike XCO, and Cyclo-Cross, may be added to the UCI Road Team Ranking.

    These additional points are included in the team ranking only if the rider is among the top 8 riders within her team’s Women Elite ranking. No points from these disciplines are added to the individual UCI Road Rankings.

    (article introduced on 19.10.26).

    Chapter X UCI RANKINGS

    § 3 Elite and Under 23 Men’s Continental Rankings

    Special Provisions for Riders Competing in Other Cycling Disciplines 2.10.025 As from season 2027, points earned at the UCI World Championships in Track, Bis Mountain Bike XCO, Cyclo-Cross, and Gravel, as well as in the final standings of the UCI World Cup or UCI Nations Cup in Track, Mountain Bike XCO, and Cyclo-Cross, may be added to the UCI Road Team Ranking.

    These additional points are included in the team ranking only if the rider is among the top 20 riders within his team’s Men Elite ranking. No points from these disciplines are added to the individual UCI Road Rankings.

    (article introduced on 19.10.26).
